## Corrections and qualifications

- The page classifies Feminist Philosophy as a plural field or family of traditions, not one doctrine or political program.
- Wollstonecraft’s 1792 *Vindication* is described as a major European landmark and Atlas date anchor, not the universal origin of feminism.
- The wave metaphor is limited by geography and the discontinuities it can hide; it is not used as a global master chronology.
- Intersectionality is explained as an intervention in single-axis legal and political framing, not a checklist or simple arithmetic of identities.
- Situated knowledge and standpoint are not presented as making identity automatically authoritative or evidence optional.
- Care ethics is separated from claims that women are naturally caring; institutional organization and unequal care labor remain visible.
- Sex, gender, embodiment, identity, social position, and political category are kept conceptually contestable; trans philosophy is part of the field rather than an optional add-on.
- Disability, Black feminist, Latin American, transnational, decolonial, Indigenous, and global work are used to challenge a single default subject without implying consensus among those traditions.
- The wall chart now labels 1792 as a European landmark rather than a universal beginning. The Feminist Philosophy/existentialism edge is `overlaps-with`, not `modern-descendant`, and its summary describes feminist existentialism as one current in a plural field.
