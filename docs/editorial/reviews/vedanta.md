## Method and boundaries

The review covers the branch article, classification, broad date anchor, origin story, historical development, core questions, concepts, works, figures, subbranches, internal debates, misunderstandings, modern relevance, and reading paths. It treats Vedānta as a contested family formed through interpretation, practice, and institutions, not as a single doctrine or an alias for Advaita. The page uses Śaṅkara, Rāmānuja, and Madhva as major comparison points while deliberately opening beyond the trio to early Vedānta, Bhedābheda, other Vaiṣṇava systems, Śaiva Vedānta, and modern receptions. Museum and other reused surfaces were reconciled separately in the same release and remain outside this article lock.

## Evidence base

The IEP Upaniṣads overview supplies the layered textual and chronological orientation. Andrew Nicholson’s IEP Bhedābheda entry corrects the three-school story and supports early and continuing plurality. His *Unifying Hinduism* historicizes doxography, system classification, and precolonial as well as modern unity projects. Ayon Maharaj’s *Bloomsbury Research Handbook of Vedānta* supplies the broad current research map. Public-domain Śaṅkara and Rāmānuja Brahma Sūtra commentaries provide inspectable comparative primary locators, while Sangeetha Menon’s IEP Advaita article prevents Advaita’s history from being silently equated with the whole family.

## Corrections and qualifications

- Roughly 800 BCE is a broad Upaniṣadic horizon, not a precise founding event for Vedānta.
- The Upaniṣads are layered, internally varied texts, and the Brahma Sūtra’s authorship, dating, and early interpretation remain difficult.
- Śaṅkara is not the founder of Vedānta, and Advaita is not the family’s default or final form.
- Bhedābheda and early interpreters break the teleological sequence from Upaniṣads directly to the famous three.
- Viśiṣṭādvaita and Dvaita are rival systems, not devotional approximations to nonduality.
- Shared vocabulary receives incompatible definitions; keyword matching is not substantive comparison.
- Philosophy, exegesis, devotion, ritual, renunciation, temple life, and institutional authority are analyzed together.
- Metaphysical unity or devotional accessibility is not treated as proof of historical social equality.
- Modern universalism, Neo-Vedānta, nationalism, therapeutic nonduality, and consciousness discourse are historically located receptions.
- Premodern projects of system unity are retained so colonial construction is not made an all-purpose origin explanation.

## Unresolved questions and lock status

The family’s exact boundary, early chronology, lost interpreter positions, school labels, corpus hierarchy, social-access histories, and relation between classical and modern Vedāntas remain contested. A concise Atlas necessarily underrepresents several living lineages; the article makes that limitation explicit rather than presenting a complete taxonomy.
