## Corrections, qualifications, and disputes

- Frege is presented as foundational for modern quantificational logic, not its uncontested sole inventor.
- The logicist project’s original derivation fails because Basic Law V is inconsistent, while the survival of separate logical and semantic innovations is stated without turning that survival into a rescue of the original system.
- The context principle is located in the *Foundations* and its scope across Frege’s career remains interpretively disputed.
- Sense and reference are not treated as a complete theory of every linguistic phenomenon, and the concept–object distinction retains its notorious expressive tension.
- Direct influence is described most securely through Russell and Wittgenstein; broader analytic reception is not converted into undocumented personal transmission.
