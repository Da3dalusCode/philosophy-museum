## Method and boundaries

The review treats Kaṇāda as the traditional author figure of a layered *Vaiśeṣika Sūtra*, not as a documented scientist with exact life dates. It maps the received sūtra, category project, substances, atoms and wholes, universals, particularizers, inherence, causal production, cognition, dharma and liberation, Praśastapāda’s reorganization, the Nyāya–Vaiśeṣika synthesis, opponents, and modern comparison. Museum labels and other reuse surfaces were reconciled separately in the same release and remain outside this article lock.

## Sources and locator inspection

Primary-text locators use the book and aphorism divisions in Nandalal Sinha’s public-domain translation, explicitly labeled a non-critical recension transmitted with Śaṅkara Miśra’s much later *Upaskāra*. Karl Potter’s specialist volume controls textual history and later development. Amita Chatterjee’s SEP entry controls comparative atomism and causation; Jonardon Ganeri’s SEP entry controls the mature category and inference system; Bimal Matilal supplies an independent historical-philosophical treatment. Later Nyāya–Vaiśeṣika material is never silently back-attributed to Kaṇāda.

## Corrections and qualifications

- The previous 600–500 BCE lifespan is removed. No reliable biography or exact floruit survives; numeric years are only visualization anchors.
- Kaṇāda, Ulūka, and “particle-eater” narratives are treated as traditional names and reception, not documentary episodes.
- The received *Vaiśeṣika Sūtra* is treated as layered and textually mediated.
- The classical six categories are distinguished from the later fully recognized seventh category, absence.
- Praśastapāda’s systematic exposition and later Nyāya–Vaiśeṣika arguments receive their own attribution.
- Vaiśeṣika atoms are eternal, element-specific, imperceptible simples within a plural ontology; they are not modern atoms or proof of experimental physics.
- The school includes enduring selves, atomic mind, moral causation, dharma, and liberation, so “materialist” is rejected.
- Early divine causation remains contested; mature theistic Nyāya arguments are not projected into every sūtra layer.

## Remaining uncertainties and integration acceptance

The identity and date of Kaṇāda, unity and order of the sūtra collection, earliest meaning of particular aphorisms, and exact transitions to Praśastapāda and combined Nyāya–Vaiśeṣika remain unresolved. The integrated record preserves low date confidence; the stored lock reflects the final reviewed snapshot.
