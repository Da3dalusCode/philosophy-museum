## Corrections, qualifications, and disputes

- “Two Dogmas” challenges leading accounts of analyticity and reductionism; it is not represented as a formal proof that no constrained analytic distinction can ever be drawn.
- The web of belief is unevenly structured: observation sentences and central theoretical commitments play different roles, so not every statement is equally revisable.
- Indeterminacy of translation is distinguished from ordinary ambiguity and from the broader underdetermination of theory by evidence.
- Bound-variable commitment is a criterion for reading a regimented theory’s commitments, not a complete substantive ontology by itself.
- Naturalized epistemology’s relation to justification and normativity remains disputed rather than being declared either a simple replacement or a category mistake.
