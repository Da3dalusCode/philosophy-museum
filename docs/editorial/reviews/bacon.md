## Corrections and uncertainties retained

- Bacon did not single-handedly invent observation, experiment, induction, or “the scientific method.”
- Induction is eliminative, staged, provisional, and reciprocal rather than mere enumeration.
- Natural history is foundational evidential labor, not indiscriminate collecting.
- The Great Instauration’s incompletion remains visible.
- The empiricist label is qualified by speculative matter theory, forms, and theological commitments.
- Collaborative science is assessed alongside hierarchy, secrecy, credit, empire, and governance.
- Salomon’s House is an unfinished fiction with several institutional afterlives, not a literal Royal Society blueprint.
- The 1621 disgrace is neither excused nor allowed to erase the intellectual program.
