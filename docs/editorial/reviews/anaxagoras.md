- Prevented Aristotle’s “homoeomeries” from appearing as Anaxagoras’s own uncontested technical category.

- Preserved the ambiguity of Nous as finest/purest yet unmixed, rather than declaring either modern materialism or mind–matter dualism.

## Corrections and uncertainties retained

- The fundamental ingredient inventory remains contested, and no modern English noun settles it.
- Complete separation is denied for ordinary ingredients; the extent and geometry of cosmic separation remain disputed.
- Nous securely knows and initiates rotation, but the extent of continuing governance and purposive choice remains open.
- Astronomy, biology, and perception rely more heavily on reports than the core mixture/Nous account.
- Conventional dates and the relationship with Pericles are useful orientations with lower confidence than the fragments.
- Plato’s teleological disappointment is indispensable reception, not a neutral abstract of the lost book.
