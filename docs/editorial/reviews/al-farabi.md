## Required corrections and uncertainty decisions

1. Replace `872–950` with `c. 870–950/51`, low confidence. F5 describes the birth as approximate and death in Damascus in December 950 or January 951. Ethnic origin, early education, and much travel chronology are disputed or late.
2. Keep “Second Teacher” as a reception title, not a contemporary measure of uncontested rank. Do not write a seamless biography from later reports.
3. Qualify the article’s Plato–Aristotle harmony section. The attribution of *Harmonization of the Opinions of the Two Sages* has been seriously challenged; it cannot anchor al-Farabi’s authorial program without a dispute label. His access to Plato was also mediated and incomplete.
4. Do not present metaphysics as one entirely stable system across the corpus. F5/F7 flag tensions between ontological, cosmological, and political works and questions of chronology/authenticity.
5. Preserve the account of logic as organon and curriculum, but identify the five argumentative arts and distinguish demonstration from dialectic, rhetoric, sophistry, and poetics. Logic does not simply replace ordinary language; F6 emphasizes their relation.
6. Qualify “religion presents philosophical truth symbolically.” In the *Book of Religion* and political works, virtuous religions represent or imitate truths for communities; this is a normative political account, permits a plurality of good representations, and is not a blanket dismissal of all religion as mere imagery.
7. Treat prophecy through the active intellect and perfected imagination as a technical political-psychological account. Do not merge it with every emanation passage or claim all texts use identical terminology.
8. Keep the Virtuous City as a normative regime ordered toward happiness, not a historical description of Baghdad or a simple authoritarian blueprint. The surviving ethical textual base is thinner than the profile implies; the reported commentary on Aristotle’s *Nicomachean Ethics* is lost.
9. The reading strategy must not imply that a full English *Enumeration of the Sciences* is readily available in F1: Butterworth translates ch. 5 there. Name the translated selection or another verified edition.
10. Make influence language route- and text-specific. Avicenna’s engagement is substantial; Latin and Jewish reception came through particular translated works and traditions, not one undifferentiated “influence.”
