## Material weaknesses corrected

- Replaced a numeric pseudo-lifespan with an explicit anonymous-author date horizon around 500.
- Distinguished the corpus from the biblical Dionysius of Acts and from later apostolic-authenticity reception.
- Treated references to lost works, headings, persona, and early scholia as parts of a mediated corpus rather than secure biography.
- Explained procession and return as non-temporal structures of causality, participation, desire, and transmission.
- Connected divine names and apophasis without reducing affirmative theology to false speech or negative theology to simple denial.
- Restored symbols, bodies, rites, and public liturgy to a profile often reduced to private mystical darkness.
- Presented hierarchy as communicative participation with real institutional and political risks, not merely an angel chart.
- Distinguished secure Proclean dependence from undocumented claims of personal study, and the late-antique corpus from its Byzantine and Latin transformations.
