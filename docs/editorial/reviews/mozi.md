- Replaced exact-life confidence with a floruit and low-confidence note. Conventional `470–391 BCE` values remain only numeric placement anchors required by the existing record type.
- Distinguished the historical teacher, organized Mohist communities, early doctrinal triads, dialogues, later Canons, and defensive-warfare chapters.

- Used “inclusive or impartial concern” as the main explanation of `jian ai`; rejected both a thin ordinary-benevolence reading and an unqualified claim of identical emotion toward everyone.
- Distinguished plural Mohist benefit from later hedonistic or preference utilitarianism and retained Heaven’s justificatory role.
- Limited the war claim to opposition to offensive aggression; defense, punishment, and centralized coercion prevent an unqualified pacifist label.
- Treated Heaven and ghosts as serious parts of the texts rather than secularizing them into knowingly useful fictions.
- Kept later Mohist semantics, epistemology, analogy, geometry, mechanics, and optics separate from Mozi’s personal authorship and from claims of modern formal logic.

## Uncertainties retained

Interpretive disputes remain over equal concern versus equal treatment, consequence versus Heaven as foundation, the epistemic force of the “three tests,” and the political consistency of inclusive care with conformity upward. The Canons remain textually difficult and reconstruction-dependent.
