- Translation, summaries, corpus arrangement, and commentary are presented as interpretive interventions.

- Hermetic antiquity is identified as a Renaissance chronological error without making prisca theologia philosophically trivial.
- Soul, love, embodiment, and cosmic hierarchy retain internal tensions.
- Three Books on Life is neither sanitized metaphor nor collapsed into demonic magic.
- Later reception distinguishes direct use, availability, selection, and broad resemblance.
