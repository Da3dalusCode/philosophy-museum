## Corrections, qualifications, and disputes

- Popper formed his view in Vienna but was not a member of the Vienna Circle.
- Falsifiability is a methodological demarcation proposal, not a guarantee of truth or a mechanical rule that one adverse result kills a theory regardless of auxiliaries and accepted basic statements.
- Corroboration records survival of severe tests and is not equated with high probability or confirmation in Carnap’s sense.
- Metaphysical ideas can be meaningful and heuristically fruitful even when not currently testable, so demarcation is not a universal meaning criterion.
- The open society is tied to criticism and corrigible institutions, not reduced to a contemporary market slogan.
- Popper’s readings of Plato, Hegel, and Marx are identified as politically and interpretively contested.
