## Required corrections and uncertainty decisions

1. Keep `980–1037` as conventional high-confidence anchors, but separate the near-contemporary autobiography/biography tradition from neutral modern reportage. Anecdotes about dictating from memory or writing while traveling need a named biographical source and genre caveat.
2. Rewrite essence/existence language so it does not imply a simple extra-mental property relation or project Aquinas’s later real distinction back into Avicenna. V1/V4 support conceptual consideration of quiddity without existential commitment; scholarly disagreement remains over how metaphysical and mental distinctions should be formulated.
3. Rewrite the Necessary Existent argument. It is not merely “every chain needs a first link” and does not infer a temporal first event. Avicenna divides existents into necessary in themselves and possible in themselves/necessary through another, then considers the causal dependence of possibles and their totality. Use V1 VIII.1–4 and V4’s modality/causality section.
4. The Flying Man establishes immediate self-awareness under radical sensory deprivation and bears on the soul considered in itself. Do not say it straightforwardly “proves the soul is immaterial” without marking the disputed inferential steps. V6 and V7 offer distinct but compatible cautions.
5. Divine knowledge of particulars is a live interpretive problem. “In a universal way” is traditional shorthand that can mislead: Avicenna does not make God ignorant of particulars, but explains knowledge without temporal change or reception from effects. Attribute the formula and map it to V1 VIII.6, VIII.7, and related providence passages; preserve dispute.
6. Identify the Greek inheritance as mediated through late-antique Arabic traditions. Do not imply Avicenna directly read a complete Plato or simply combined Aristotle and Plotinus.
7. Keep the medical and logical breadth, but do not use a late manuscript as evidence that a particular medical claim is correct. Distinguish composition of the *Canon*, its manuscript/print reception, and its later curricular authority.
8. Replace the exact 1020 timeline point with `early eleventh century` or `c. 1020s` unless a specific work-redaction is being dated. Essence/existence is distributed across works, not a single datable discovery event.
9. Relationship claims to al-Farabi, al-Ghazali, Averroes, Maimonides, and Latin scholastics should identify whether they are sources, critics, readers, or later reception. Avoid a single undifferentiated influence chain.
