## Method and boundary

The replacement reviews every article paragraph and all claim-bearing philosopher facts. It covers uncertain chronology, traditional biography, Gandhāran and North Indian setting, Abhidharma and Yogācāra contexts, works and attribution, no-self and continuity, appearance only, atom and percept arguments, three natures, hermeneutics, multilingual transmission, influence, misconceptions, and reading routes. It does not review every later Abhidharma or Yogācāra school or every work attributed to Vasubandhu. Museum and other reused surfaces were reconciled separately in the same release and remain outside this article lock.

## Sources and locator inspection

The central specialist authority is Jonathan C. Gold’s SEP entry, whose sections on disputed biography, the Treasury, scripture, Yogācāra arguments, and idealism were inspected. Primary evidence uses Jonathan Gold’s open translation of the *Twenty Verses with Auto-Commentary*, the GRETIL Sanskrit *Abhidharmakośabhāṣya* record, and the University of Hawai‘i Press record for Francis Cook’s translations. Jaini’s Cambridge journal article establishes the historical two-author dispute without being treated as its final settlement. Coseru’s SEP article supplies an independent tradition-level control.

## Corrections and qualifications

- Exact years 316–396 are replaced by `c. 4th–5th century CE; chronology uncertain`.
- Traditional travel, court, debate, and conversion stories are not presented as secure dated biography.
- Vaibhāṣika, Sautrāntika, and Yogācāra name argumentative contexts and disputed trajectories, not a simple three-stage institutional career.
- Scholarly movement away from a strict two-author theory does not authenticate the whole traditional corpus.
- The *Abhidharmakośabhāṣya* is treated as exposition and criticism, not a neutral statement of everything Vasubandhu believed.
- `Vijñaptimātra` is not reduced to “nothing exists except my mind”; idealist, phenomenalist, epistemic, and therapeutic readings remain contested.
- The *Thirty Verses* is kept distinct from Xuanzang’s later *Demonstration of Consciousness Only*.
- Sanskrit, Chinese, Tibetan, and commentarial mediation is part of the philosophical record.

## Open questions and integration assumptions

The exact chronology, one-versus-two-author reconstruction, attribution of individual works, and relation between Abhidharma and Yogācāra corpora remain open. The integrated record preserves those uncertainties, and the stored deterministic lock reflects its final reviewed snapshot.
