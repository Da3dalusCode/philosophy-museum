## Findings and corrections

- The conventional chronology and court travels remain approximate; stories of Mencius’s mother are identified as later exemplary biography.
- The *Mengzi* is a layered dialogue and argument collection, not a verbatim itinerary.
- “Human nature is good” means neither completed virtue nor guaranteed good choice. The profile explains fragile, directionally organized capacities requiring nourishment and extension.
- The four beginnings are treated as integrated affective-evaluative responses rather than four modern faculties or infallible impulses.
- Environment, livelihood, habit, reflection, agency, and institutional responsibility remain connected.
- `Qi` is not converted into a measurable occult substance, and desire is not opposed simplistically to reason.
- Humane government includes material security and conditional legitimacy, but is not mislabeled modern electoral sovereignty.
- Mencius’s representations of Gaozi, Mohists, and Yang Zhu are marked as polemical evidence.
- The Mencius–Xunzi disagreement is preserved as a dispute over nature, achieved goodness, capacities, and classification—not an optimism/pessimism slogan.
