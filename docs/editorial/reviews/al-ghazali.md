## Required corrections and uncertainty decisions

1. Replace `1058–1111` with `c. 1055/56–1111` (medium confidence) and explain the dispute. G4’s revised chronology reports 1058/59 in later biographical tradition but 1055/56 from al-Ghazali’s letters and autobiographical chronology.
2. Correct the article and Museum treatment of *Aims of the Philosophers* (*Maqasid*). The older story that it was a neutral preparatory synopsis written simply to set up the *Incoherence* cannot be upheld. G4 §2 identifies it as an Arabic adaptation of Avicenna’s Persian *Danishnama* and finds the connection to the *Incoherence* loose; the explicit framing may be later than the main text.
3. State the structure of the *Incoherence*: twenty discussions, with a concluding judgment that three positions cross into unbelief (world’s pre-eternity, denial that God knows particulars in the relevant sense, and denial of bodily resurrection) while other errors are treated differently. Do not describe a blanket condemnation of reason, logic, medicine, mathematics, or philosophy.
4. Rewrite causal claims. Discussion 17 attacks the claim that the observed conjunction is necessary in itself, but al-Ghazali’s works remain compatible with more than one explanatory model: direct occasional creation and secondary causes governed by a stable divine order. “Habit of divine action” alone overstates scholarly settlement; G4 §§3–4 and G5 chs. 6–9 preserve the live dispute.
5. Keep his positive use of logic, but name contexts and works where possible (*Criterion of Knowledge*, *Just Balance*, *The Standard/Touchstone*, legal theory). Logic is not identical with acceptance of Avicennian metaphysics.
6. Treat *Deliverance from Error* as crafted retrospective self-presentation. The Baghdad crisis, departure, and Sufi discipline are central, but motive, duration, and “seclusion” should be checked against letters and contemporary evidence in G4/G5.
7. Keep the integration of law, Ashʿari theology, ethics, and Sufi practice. Do not flatten him into either an anti-rational mystic or a covert Avicennian. The degree and function of Avicennian appropriation are disputed.
8. Preserve the rejection of the “ended Islamic philosophy” myth and strengthen it with G7. Averroes’s response is important but not the sole measure of al-Ghazali’s reception.
9. Make the 1095 timeline date approximate and avoid saying one book fixed a permanent “boundary between reason and revelation.” 1095 is also the year of departure from Baghdad; work chronology needs work-specific evidence.
