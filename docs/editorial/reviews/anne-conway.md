## Corrections, qualifications, and disputes

- Created reality is presented as living spiritual substance without turning it into one undifferentiated cosmic mind.
- God, Christ or middle nature, and creatures are three species distinguished by mutability; creatures do not cross into the divine species.
- Body is a grosser mode of living created substance, not an illusion or wholly alien inert substance.
- Creaturely plurality, nested organization, unity in multiplicity, and moral responsibility are preserved alongside the unity of created substance.
- Evil is a creaturely deterioration rather than a substance; suffering is presented as purgative within Conway’s theodicy, with the ethical difficulty of that account kept visible.
- Platonist, Origenist, Kabbalistic, medical, and Quaker contexts are treated as mediated influences, not one master key.
- Affinity and possible influence on Leibniz are distinguished from the false claim that the *Principles* is an early draft of the *Monadology*.
