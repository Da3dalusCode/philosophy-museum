## Corrections, qualifications, and disputes

- Russell’s views are periodized: logicism, acquaintance, logical atomism, neutral-monist directions, and later public writing are connected but not flattened.
- *Principia Mathematica* is credited jointly with Whitehead; ramified type theory and the axiom of reducibility prevent a simple story of an unqualified foundational victory.
- The theory of descriptions is presented as an analysis of a major class of denoting constructions, not a universal recipe for language.
- Acquaintance and logical atomism retain their internal and historical difficulties rather than being presented as settled results.
- Russell’s antiwar activity is distinguished by period and circumstance, and the 1950 Nobel Prize is correctly identified as Literature rather than Philosophy or Peace.
