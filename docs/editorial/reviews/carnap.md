## Corrections, qualifications, and disputes

- Carnap is a central Vienna Circle participant, not its sole founder or uncontested leader.
- The *Aufbau* is not reduced to a crude translation of every claim into private sensations; its basis and constructional ambitions remain disputed.
- Criticism of metaphysics is presented as a program of logical and linguistic diagnosis, not a universally accepted proof that every metaphysical question is meaningless.
- Tolerance concerns choice among rule-governed frameworks for purposes; it does not imply that all frameworks are equally useful or empirically adequate.
- External questions are distinguished from internal questions without calling every external issue meaningless; Quine’s objections transform but do not simply refute Carnap in one step.
- *Logical Syntax* is dated 1934 in German and 1937 in revised English translation.
