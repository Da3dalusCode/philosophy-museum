## Corrections and qualifications

- Laozi is an attributed and legendary persona, not a securely documented person with birth and death years. The numeric record values are explicitly textual-formation anchors.
- Sima Qian’s archivist, Confucius-meeting, and frontier-pass narrative is reported as later tradition that itself preserves competing identities.
- The Daodejing is treated as a layered textual tradition. “Composite” does not imply random incoherence, and no exact redaction sequence is asserted as settled.
- The 81-chapter received arrangement is distinguished from Mawangdui’s De-before-Dao order and Guodian’s shorter, differently ordered selections.
- `Dao` remains way, course, and generative path rather than being fixed as a personal creator, metaphysical substance, or one translation-dependent capitalized object.
- `De` is explained as virtue, potency, and efficacy; `wu` and `you` remain context-sensitive absence/presence or not-having/having terms rather than automatic proof of creation from absolute nothing.
- `Wuwei` is noncoercive or non-self-assertive action, not literal inactivity. `Ziran` is being-so-of-itself or unforced becoming, not impulsiveness or a simple Western nature/culture opposition.
- Political passages are allowed to sustain rival counsel, critical, and statecraft readings. The profile neither converts the text into a modern libertarian platform nor erases its repeated address to rulers.
- “Daoism” is presented as a retrospective classification with deeply intertwined philosophical and religious histories, not a uniform school founded in one institutional act.

## Translation, quotation, and unresolved questions

No translated verse is quoted as if it had one neutral English form. Standard chapter locators permit checking across translations, and the reading path asks visitors to compare named versions. Unresolved questions include whether a historical Lao Dan anchors any layer; whether Guodian is a selection, nucleus, or set of teaching collections; the relation among political, mystical, ethical, cosmological, and therapeutic readings; and whether political noninterference protects people or can leave injustice intact.
