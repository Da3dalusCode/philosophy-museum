## Corrections and uncertainties retained

- Rejects “the ends justify the means” as a Machiavellian quotation or sufficient doctrine.
- Keeps analysis, rhetorical provocation, conditional prescription, and endorsement distinct.
- Defines virtù as adaptive political capacity, not conventional virtue or simple ruthlessness.
- Treats fortuna as contingency that preparation can manage but never master.
- Makes republican liberty, public speech, tumults, corruption, law, arms, and renewal central.
- Distinguishes institutionally channeled conflict from faction and civil war.
- Preserves the tension between liberty at home and expansive domination abroad.
- Leaves unity, piety, satire, republicanism, realism, and moral intention open where scholarship remains divided.
