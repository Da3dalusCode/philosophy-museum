## Corrections, distinctions, and uncertainty audit

- The “felicific calculus” is described as a set of morally and legislatively relevant circumstances, not a routinely executable algorithm that settles every choice.
- Analytical jurisprudence (what law, duty, right, and command are), normative reform (which laws institutions ought to adopt), and later Benthamite organizations are kept distinct.
- Punishment is itself an evil requiring prospective justification. Deterrence, disablement, reformation, and compensation belong to a proportional scheme; Bentham is not presented as celebrating incarceration.
- His attack is on natural or imprescriptible rights used without institutional specification, not on enforceable legal protections in every sense.
- Panopticon history distinguishes Bentham’s unbuilt British penitentiary scheme, transportation to New South Wales, Millbank Prison, and Foucault’s later disciplinary reception.
- The sexual-liberty manuscripts are philosophically radical but were not a successful public nineteenth-century reform campaign. Composition, nonpublication, later editions, and reception remain visible.
- Mature democratic commitments are not projected without qualification across his whole career. Suffrage details, gender exclusions, and timing require work-level locators.
- His criticism of costly empire and transportation is not converted into a complete anticolonial theory; offers to codify for other societies retain the asymmetry of European expertise.
- Poor-law and evidence projects expose suffering and technical exclusion while also creating systems of inspection and administrative legibility. “Humane intention” is not treated as proof of humane operation.
- The animal-suffering note expands consideration through sentience but does not answer later disputes about comparison, rights, uncertainty, or institutional practice.

No exact quotation is retained in the proposed new prose. Existing paraphrases should remain paraphrases; if the famous natural-rights or animal note is quoted during integration, a page/standard-division locator must be added.
