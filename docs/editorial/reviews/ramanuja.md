## Corrections and qualifications

- The traditional 1017–1137 lifespan is retained as a low-confidence convention; proposed alternatives such as 1077–1157 are named.
- Yāmuna and earlier Vedānta inheritances prevent a solitary-founder narrative.
- Viśiṣṭādvaita is presented as a rival nonduality of the differentiated, not an incomplete midpoint between Advaita and Dvaita.
- Souls and matter are real and dependent as brahman’s body; the analogy does not erase their characteristics or make God deficient without them.
- Consciousness is attributed to enduring knowers, and realism is argued rather than described merely as common sense.
- Śrī and temple embodiment are contextualized without attributing every later theological formulation to Rāmānuja.
- Bhakti in the major works and later prapatti traditions are distinguished; later Vaṭakalai/Teṉkalai debates are not projected fully formed into his lifetime.
- Claims of uniformly egalitarian reform are treated one episode at a time against the restrictive eligibility assumptions also present in the archive.
- The *Gadyatraya* and minor works retain genre and authorship cautions.
