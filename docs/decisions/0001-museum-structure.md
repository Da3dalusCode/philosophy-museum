# 0001 — Museum structure

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-01

## Decision

The permanent intellectual structure is **Worlds with a Questions Forum**, using the approved culturally safeguarded 10-wing, 26-gallery, 105-room program and defensible primary homes. This curatorial principle does not imply a Ring topology.

The accepted and permanent physical architecture is the currently implemented **Continuous Enfilade**. Its current masterplan, building implementation, and physical visitor map are authoritative. The architecture intentionally contains 26 curated/open galleries, 105 rooms, one chronological visitor route, six crosscut intersections and shortcuts, an integrated Core Questions Forum, a defined Grand Entrance, final return and exit, and reserves within the established plan.

This architecture is locked. Preserve it unless the owner explicitly opens a new Museum-architecture redesign. The earlier Single-level Ring of Wings is superseded planning history: it is not a future target, accepted backlog, or reason to reorganize the Museum.

## Rationale

The intellectual program protects historical and cultural context while enabling comparison. After evaluating several alternatives, the owner separately completed and approved the dimensioned Continuous Enfilade. It provides a complete chronological route while its crosscut, shortcuts, Forum, entrance, final return, exit, and reserves supply orientation and choice without reopening the settled architecture.

## Consequences

- The [recommended program](../museum-masterplan/recommended-program.md), [classification principles](../museum-masterplan/classification-principles.md), and stable assignments remain authoritative.
- The [Continuous Enfilade control plan](../museum-masterplan/single-level-building-plan.md), implemented manifest, and physical visitor map remain authoritative.
- Ring documents and diagrams may be preserved as historical evidence but must be labeled superseded.
- This governance task changes no Museum geometry, gallery arrangement, manifest, routing, or implementation.

## Accepted wayfinding amendment — 2026-08-03

The locked architecture remains unchanged, but its public wayfinding contract is clarified:

- Public Gallery 01–26 numbers are the recommended physical visitor-route order. Stable internal hall IDs remain the durable software identity and are nonpublic.
- Visitor maps omit collection-planning, construction-state, manifest, and release-tracking metadata.
- A required click to restore walking and looking uses one centered **Resume Visit** overlay derived from the actual input-capture state.
- The recommended route is a continuous, flush, noncolliding architectural floor inlay; the crosscut and shortcuts use quieter connected branches.
- Gallery 01 is governed by the established exhibit-placement standard without changing its four room identities or canonical content assignments.
- Doorway or distant-room rendering, gallery residency, and transition-hitch optimization were not reopened by this amendment.

This amendment changes presentation, interaction, and hall-local placement only. It does not reopen the 10-wing, 26-gallery, 105-room architecture, physical route, crosscut, turn courts, reserves, stable IDs, or content assignments.

## Reopening conditions

Reopen only when the owner explicitly opens a new Museum-architecture redesign. Ordinary Museum maintenance, gallery curation, content work, suggestions, and historical Ring references do not reopen this decision.
