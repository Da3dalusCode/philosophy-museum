# 0003 — Research-first content cadence

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-01

## Decision

Substantive content work follows a research-first cadence: define scope and risk; inspect the canonical record and reuse surfaces; gather appropriate primary and reputable scholarly sources; map high-risk claims and locators; draft or revise original prose; then run the validation level for the task’s actual category. Research notes and batch plans are temporary working material, not automatic standards changes.

## Rationale

Separating research, writing, mapping, and validation reduces invented precision, bibliography laundering, retrospective classifications, and prose shaped mainly to satisfy a counter.

## Consequences

- Claim-level work uses the [editorial data model](../editorial/editorial-model.md).
- Batch ordering may follow the [editorial credibility roadmap](../editorial/editorial-credibility-roadmap.md), but live inventories determine current gaps.
- Validation follows the [matrix](../standards/validation-matrix.md), not a blanket browser or full-suite ritual.

## Reopening conditions

Reopen if a documented editorial workflow produces equally strong evidence integrity with less operational cost, and the owner explicitly approves the standards change.
